**21 May 2026** — Two new releases pushed to [qmnet](https://github.com/QHDALabs/qmnet).

`rqte_v3.py` is now live — a full implementation of Relational Quantum
Temporal Encryption built on Page-Wootters formalism and
measurement-fueled bridges. Three irreversibility classes, correct
encryption and decryption confirmed, technical note published.

The bridge experiment codebase (`qmnet_v3.py`, `qmnet_v4.py`) is
cleaned up and stable. Both technical notes are in the repo as PDF.

A short readable summary of the RQTE work is on the
[blog](https://github.com/QHDALabs/QHDALabs/blob/0765b0ffa13022e8ded7cb068c6bb5c046f4f453/digital-garden/blog/2026-05-21-genesis-digital-garden.md). The honest version — including the result
that didn't go as expected (BUTTERFLY entropy 0.811, not 1.0).

Next: NIST randomness tests on the keystream, and a first QPU run.
