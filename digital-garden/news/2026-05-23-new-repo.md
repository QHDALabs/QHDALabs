**23 May 2026** — New repository opened:
[QHDALabs-RTANA](https://github.com/QHDALabs/QHDALabs-RTANA).

The question: can a neural architecture carry an internal *relational* clock — time constituted
by relations between its own states — rather than an external timestamp? It follows from the
[qmnet](https://github.com/QHDALabs/qmnet) result that a mid-circuit measurement creates a
relational fact with a deterministic downstream effect.

**Current state: written notes only.** The repository contains a manifesto, an open-questions
list and a research log. There is no code. The architecture proposal, proof of concept and
evaluation protocol are all unstarted, and the falsifiable test that would distinguish an
internal relational clock from a well-fitted positional encoding does not exist yet. Designing
that test comes before writing any implementation.
