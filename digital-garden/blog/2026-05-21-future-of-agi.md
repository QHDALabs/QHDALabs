# The future of AGI

**Status: draft — opinion, not research.** Early notes on the direction of AGI development, its
integration with social systems, and the ethical questions that follow.

This post is commentary. It reports no experiment, is backed by no work in this lab, and should
not be read as a research position. The one adjacent project,
[QHDALabs-RTANA](https://github.com/QHDALabs/QHDALabs-RTANA), is explicitly *not* about machine
consciousness — it asks a narrow structural question about temporal representation, and it has no
implementation yet.
